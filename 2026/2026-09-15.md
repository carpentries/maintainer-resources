### 1\. Updates from Carpentries team

* [Changes to Core Team and Programmatic Activities at The Carpentries](https://carpentries.org/blog/2026/08/changes-to-core-team-and-programmatic-activities-at-the-carpentries/)

  * [Related follow-up post from Toby](https://carpentries.org/blog/2026/08/upcoming-changes-to-the-carpentries-curriculum-program/)
  * Erin Becker's departure means that Curriculum Team capacity has been reduced. Toby remains.
  * Unfortunately, we will skip the Maintainer Onboarding cycle in 2027. We would be greatly helped by current Maintainers committing to remain in their role until the next Onboarding cycle in the first half of 2028 (or beyond!), but Maintainers will be able to step away whenever they need to via the existing offboarding process (triggered by sending an email to José and/or Toby).

    * If you do not have the capacity to continue, please tell us and do not try to stick around at risk of burning yourself out. We want to support you.
  * These regular Maintainer meetings will continue, thanks in large part to José <3
* HPC Carpentry adoption was delayed until 1 Nov. We are on target for that revised launch date.
* Planned work to roll out `CITATION.cff` files on official lesson repositories are now on hold. Toby plans to pick that work back up in 2027, pending the completion of more urgent work.
* Two Data Carpentry curricula have been retired: Geospatial and Astronomy. This difficult decision was made to free up Curriculum Team capacity. Toby wrote more about this in the second blog post linked above. Some Maintainers from the retired lessons have volunteered to be reassigned to other lessons -- thank you to them and all the other Maintainers of the affected lessons!
* The Board of Directors published [a Resolution calling for Strategic Renewal of the Curriculum Portfolio](https://carpentries.org/blog/2026/08/strategic-renewal-of-the-carpentries-curriculum-portfolio/)

  * Toby will begin coordinating task forces of community members to identify and implement updates that will improve lessons and ensure they are current and relevant. More details of this next month, when Toby will start reaching out to Maintainers and other community members, inviting them to join these task forces. Please contact him directly if already know that you would be interested in contributing.
  * Maintainers can expect some larger pull requests to review when the task forces get going. We request your support in getting the changes merged into lessons quickly -- without skipping the necessary quality control etc!
