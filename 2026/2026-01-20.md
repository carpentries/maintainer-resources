#### 2. Updates from Carpentries team
* New workflows for Workbench lesson build & maintenance!
    * (As foreshadowed in previous meetings)
    * New GitHub Actions workflows will improve build consistency and maintenance of R Markdown-based lessons, as well as enable other features
      * For official, lab and incubator lessons, storage of renv caches in AWS S3 to improve long-term reproducibility
      * Reverting to previous sandpaper versions and renv caches
      * Pinning of packages in renv lockfiles will now work
      * No need to manage SANDPAPER_WORKFLOW tokens any more
    * If your repository receives/received a PR for new workflow files today, please merge as usual
      * Due to a flag not being set by default in the old workflows, existing workflow yaml files were not cleaned out and fully replaced
      * If you merge the PR, you will have both the old and new versions of the workflows
      * This will not break existing runs, but will produce a large amount of duplicated action runs
      * Rob is working on a fix:
        * Temporarily force the option to clean workflows in the [composite action underneath](https://github.com/carpentries/actions/blob/main/update-workflows/action.yml#L15)?
    * (If you are also maintaining your own Workbench lesson repositories elsewhere, you will need to [enable the automated pull requests](https://docs.carpentries.org/resources/curriculum/lesson-forks.html#configure-maintenance-workflows) or run [the `sandpaper::update_github_workflows()` function](https://carpentries.github.io/sandpaper/reference/update_github_workflows.html) on your lesson locally and push the changes to your GitHub repository.)
    * The container images are hosted on [GHCR](https://github.com/carpentries/workbench-docker/pkgs/container/workbench-docker) and [DockerHub](https://hub.docker.com/r/carpentries/workbench-docker), and maintained by The Carpentries team. 
        * Depending on your operating system, you may find it easier/preferable to make use of those images to [run the Workbench on your own computer with Docker](https://carpentries.github.io/workbench-docs/#id_101-docker), or using [devcontainers within your GitHub lesson repository](https://carpentries.github.io/workbench-docs/devcontainer.html) itself.
    * Thanks to Hugo Gruson and Raniere Silva for help with testing the workflows :blue_heart:
    * If you have any issues at all, please let Rob Davey know on Slack in the #workbench, #lesson-dev, or #maintainers channels!

#### 4. Special Topic - New Year, Fresh Goals: Tackling the Backlog and Shaping Issues

- Have you set goals for your lesson you and your co-maintainers want to accomplish before the current maintainer cycle finishes in early April?
- Reviewing the [Maintainer Handbook](https://docs.carpentries.org/handbooks/maintainers.html) for recommendations and quick wins
    - Use the [Message Templates](https://docs.carpentries.org/handbooks/maintainers.html#message-templates-for-maintainers), could be useful to close stale PRs!
    - Rule of thumb: Feel free to make the decision you see appropriate after three weeks of inactivity on the issue/PR
    - Adding "help wanted" or "good first issue" for community help. Issues are listed here:  https://carpentries.org/lessons/help_wanted/
- Skill-Up: The Art of "Issue Shaping". Bring one stale or vague issue from your repository, see if rewriting the issue title and body is suitable, and apply appropriate labels.
